## 📖 What Is Applied Energistics 2?

**Applied Energistics 2 (AE2)** is a storage and logistics mod that implements a Matter-Energy (ME) network system. It adds components and mechanics for digital storage, automation, and autocrafting [citation:16].

The mod is designed to work with and allow automation of other mods in a modpack. You can set up your system to, with a single click, craft all of the prerequisites (and the final result) of a complex crafting chain, or keep certain quantities of items in stock, crafting more as needed [citation:1].

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **Digital storage** — Store thousands of items in compact drives
- ✅ **ME Terminals** — Searchable, unified access to all storage
- ✅ **Autocrafting** — Single-click crafting of complex chains
- ✅ **Channel system** — Network bandwidth management
- ✅ **Mod integration** — Automate machines from other mods
- ✅ **Fluid support** — Store and manage fluids digitally

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What is Applied Energistics 2?**
A: A storage and automation mod that creates digital ME Networks for storing, searching, and autocrafting items and fluids [citation:9].

**Q: Is AE2 free?**
A: Yes — 100% free and open-source. Can be freely redistributed in non-commercial modpacks [citation:2].

**Q: What Minecraft versions are supported?**
A: **1.18.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1, 1.20.4, and 1.21.1**. Support varies by version [citation:17].

**Q: Forge, NeoForge, or Fabric?**
A: All three are supported. NeoForge is used for newer versions [citation:2].

**Q: What does AE2 stand for?**
A: Applied Energistics 2 — the second major version of the original Applied Energistics mod [citation:9].

**Q: Does AE2 need power?**
A: Yes — the ME Network requires continuous power. AE2 has its own energy system ("AE") with native generators [citation:9].

**Q: How does storage work?**
A: Items are stored in Storage Cells inside Drives. Cells have 1k, 4k, 16k, and 64k tiers. You cannot upgrade existing cells — craft higher tiers instead [citation:9].

**Q: How do I autocraft?**
A: You need a Pattern Encoding Terminal, Molecular Assemblers, Pattern Providers, and a Crafting CPU. Encode patterns, place them in providers, and request crafts from your terminal [citation:4][citation:7].

**Q: What are Channels?**
A: Channels are network bandwidth. Standard cables support 8 channels; Dense cables support 32. A Controller is needed for networks exceeding 8 devices [citation:6].

**Q: What are P2P Tunnels?**
A: Point-to-Point tunnels transfer channels, items, fluids, redstone, and more. ME P2P can carry up to 32 channels [citation:13].

**Q: What are Meteorites?**
A: World-generated craters containing Skystone, Certus Quartz, and Inscriber Presses — essential for early AE2 progression.

**Q: Does it work with other mods?**
A: Yes — AE2 is designed to automate machines from other mods using Processing Patterns [citation:7].

**Q: Does it work on Bedrock / mobile?**
A: No — **Java Edition only**.

**Q: How do I uninstall it?**
A: Delete the `.jar` from `.minecraft/mods/` and restart.
